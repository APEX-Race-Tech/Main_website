What Race Insight does

Open a logger file, find the session, compare laps, then take the results with you.

1 Import 2 Sessions 3 Analysis 4 Workbook 5 Export

Step 1 · Import

Open the files your logger already writes

Drop a file into Race Insight. It is read on your computer.

  • Large files, 200 MB and up, are ready in seconds.
  • Gaps in the data stay visible. Missing samples are never filled with made-up values.
  • After your first sign-in, import and analysis work offline. Map tiles need a connection.

Supported formats

  • iRacing .ibt
  • MoTeC .ld, .ldx
  • AiM .xrk
  • VBOX .vbo, .vbb
  • Autosport Labs .log
  • CSV .csv
  • Spreadsheets .xlsx, .xls, .ods

Step 2 · Sessions

Find the session you want

  • Filter by track, driver or vehicle. Sort by date, lap count or fastest lap.
  • Each session shows its average lap, consistency and theoretical best.
  • The lap list, track map and lap-time trend sit on one screen.

Step 3 · Analysis

Compare laps and see where the time goes

  • Overlay any laps by distance or time, even ones recorded at different rates.
  • Move the cursor on one graph and every graph, value and the track map follow.
  • See your line on satellite imagery or a vector map.
  • Next release Math channels with derivative, integral, smoothing and lookup functions.
  • Next release Your own track splits, with sector times and a theoretical best lap.

Step 4 · Workbook

Lay out the screen the way you work

  • Drag, resize and arrange graphs, track maps, speed dials, throttle and brake bars, tyre monitors and G-force gauges.
  • Next release Lap analysis widgets: corner map, braking and acceleration zones, corner comparison, lap consistency, sector analysis, suspension velocity and tyre grip.
  • Next release A session report with every lap analysis in one place, and a coaching card that shows the corners where you lose the most time.

Step 5 · Export

Take your data to other tools

  • Export CSV for Excel, MATLAB or Python. Each channel keeps its own sample rate, and the file imports back without loss.
  • Next release Parquet export.
  • Next release A printable HTML report and a summary JSON with lap times and best lap.
  • Next release Graphs as PNG images and track maps as SVG files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Race Insight — compatibility, pricing, and features.

What is Race Insight?

Race Insight is a professional-grade motorsport data analysis application by APEXRACE Technologies. It lets sim racers and real-world racers import telemetry data, compare laps side-by-side, analyze speed, throttle, brake and steering channels, and build custom workbooks with lap analysis widgets.

Which racing simulators does Race Insight support?

Race Insight currently supports iRacing telemetry (.ibt), with more simulators planned. It also imports session files from real-world data loggers: MoTeC, AiM, VBOX and Autosport Labs.

What racing data formats can Race Insight import?

Race Insight imports iRacing (.ibt), MoTeC (.ld, .ldx), AiM (.xrk), VBOX (.vbo, .vbb), Autosport Labs (.log), CSV, and Excel or OpenDocument spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls, .ods).

Is Race Insight free?

The RACE Insight early access licence costs US$15 one time before tax (regular price US$35) and includes version 1 when released. Buy it in the Download section of this page; the installer downloads straight after payment. Sign in with the same RACE Insight account you use in the desktop app.

What are the system requirements for Race Insight?

Race Insight is available for Windows. Linux and macOS versions are coming soon. Import and analysis work offline after your first sign-in; map tiles require an internet connection.

Can I compare laps in Race Insight?

Yes. Race Insight lets you compare any laps side-by-side, even when recorded at different sample rates. Laps are automatically aligned so you can see exactly where you gained or lost time.

Can I export my analysis from Race Insight?

Yes. Race Insight exports your data to CSV. The next release adds lossless native CSV, Parquet, HTML session reports, Summary JSON, PNG graph images and SVG track maps.
